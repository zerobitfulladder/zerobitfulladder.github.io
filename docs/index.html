Tuğrul Ağrikli

They call me Tuğrul.

Computer science graduate, based in Istanbul. I build systems at the level below the one the problem showed up on — frameworks rather than apps, pipelines rather than datasets, a shell rather than a desktop.

Most of what I work on sits where deterministic control meets something stochastic: a scripted spine with an improvised surface, a planner checked against a learned policy, an LLM given a rendering slot but never the steering wheel. I've found myself interested in cognition — its existence, and its realizability through computation — and in flying things.
